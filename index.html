Excerpts from the Preface

"Education is not just about the classroom; it's about the potential unlocked when a single person is given the tools to dream big and the plan to execute those dreams."

Over the past twenty years, the Wells Mountain Initiative has been more than a scholarship program. It has been a testament to the power of belief in human potential. From humble beginnings in 2005 to a global network of changemakers today, this story belongs to every student who dared to look beyond their circumstances.

As we reflect on 2005-2025, 'Dream, Plan, Do!' serves as both a history and a roadmap for the future of global philanthropy and community-led development.

Thomas M. Wells
Founder, Wells Mountain Initiative

About the Author

Thomas M. Wells is the founder and heart behind the Wells Mountain Initiative. A philanthropist with a radical vision, Tom has dedicated two decades to bridging the gap in global education access.

His journey has taken him across continents, meeting Scholars in their home villages, and building a global family that transcends borders and cultures. This book is his personal account of those 20 years—the challenges, the triumphs, and the enduring belief that education is the most powerful tool for social change.
